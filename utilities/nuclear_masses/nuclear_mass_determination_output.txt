This script calculates masses of light nuclei (in atomic units, i.e. in the electron masses) including error estimates.
The values of the fundamental constants are taken from CODATA 2022 recommended values:
    P. Mohr, D. Newell, B. Taylor, and E. Tiesinga
    CODATA Recommended Values of the Fundamental Physical Constants: 2022
    arXiv:2409.03787 [hep-ph]
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.03787
Atomic weights are taken from AME 2020 atomic mass evaluation
    Wang et al., Chin. Physics C 45, 030003 (2021)
    https://dx.doi.org/10.1088/1674-1137/abddaf
Most common isotopic compositions are avaialble at this NIST website
    https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/Compositions/stand_alone.pl
